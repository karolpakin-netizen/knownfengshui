Our story

A cultural tradition, approached with care and curiosity.

Known Feng Shui exists to make an ancient Chinese space-arrangement philosophy approachable for modern Western homes, apartments, and rental living. We translate thoughtful observation of rooms into practical, calm choices—without fear-based language, fortune-telling, or promises.

Why we exist

Space affects how daily life feels.

Feng shui is an ancient Chinese home-arrangement philosophy: a way of noticing how people, place, movement, light, and purpose relate. It is not astrology, divination, or a method for predicting a person’s future. We preserve that distinction so the tradition can be explored with cultural respect.

We created Known Feng Shui for people living in modern apartments, rentals, shared spaces, family homes, and home offices. Each guide turns broad ideas into practical reflection that can be adapted to different bodies, cultures, budgets, accessibility needs, leases, and tastes.

The aim is not to make a room “perfect.” It is to help you see it more clearly, support everyday comfort and function, and make intentional choices at a realistic pace.

What guides our work

Clear principles, quietly practiced.

01

Cultural respect

We present feng shui as a Chinese cultural and spatial philosophy, not as an exotic shortcut or marketing promise.

02

Practical agency

Readers are invited to observe, test, adapt, and decide. No rigid formula should override safety or lived needs.

03

Honest education

We do not tell fortunes, claim spiritual healing, predict outcomes, or guarantee changes in health, wealth, or relationships.

What we offer

Educational resources, delivered digitally.

We only sell digital educational materials: self-paced PDF guides, printable study tools, learning bundles, and optional room-layout review reports. We do not provide destiny readings, astrology, fortune-telling, spiritual healing, or guaranteed results, and we ship no physical products.
